  • About

    I'm a theory CS postdoc (Motwani Fellow) at Stanford, hosted by Mary Wootters and Tselil Schramm. In Jan 2027, I'll join Imperial College London's Department of Computing as an Assistant Professor.

    I received my Ph.D. from the University of Chicago, advised by Madhur Tulsiani and Janos Simon.

    My research interests include pseudorandomness, (high-dimensional) expansion, quantum error correction, and anything sufficiently seasoned with algebra!
